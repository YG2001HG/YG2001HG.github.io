Qualcomm SA Project

Class role-play: pitching as a Qualcomm solutions architect

  • Role: Scenario & business design
  • Where: GPBL Cloud course
  • When: Oct – Dec 2026 (final presentation Dec 11)

We pitch a camera for a telecom's elder-care service that makes its call inside the device. Video stays in the home; the family gets only the alert.

What I did

I proposed the whole scenario. My teammates set up the AI models on the board.

  • Scenario: a 3-month pilot in 30 homes that already use the telecom's home camera, with our board added. If it works, a new care camera follows. The main argument is privacy; seeing meals and medication, which radar can't, is the supporting point.
  • Payback math: a first pass showed today's demo board can't pay for itself on a monthly care fee. So the pilot runs on development boards that Qualcomm covers, and production needs a cheaper chip. I'm still refining these numbers.
  • Customer reaction design: expected questions and answers for each person in the meeting, product planning and engineering. For example, "You chose radar over cameras. Why go back to a camera?" Our answer: "Radar won because video left the home. Now it doesn't."

Still working on

How to make people comfortable with a camera at home even when video never leaves it, and how US and Korean users differ.

Teammates' part

Pose detection on the board (17 joints, about 15 fps) and the cloud connection.
