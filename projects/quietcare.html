QuietCare (working name): Privacy-First Elder Care for Smart Homes

  • Focus: Business & Solution Planning
  • Type: Team project, in progress
  • Where: GPBL Cloud course
  • When: 2026

An on-device AI camera for elder care at home. It sends alerts, not raw video.

This is a class role-play. Our team plays Qualcomm solutions architects pitching a fictional customer, LG U+. It is not a real product or client engagement. “QuietCare” is a working name, and the scenario and numbers are still drafts that the team has not finalized.

What I did

  • Proposed the problem and detection scope: falls, long periods of inactivity, meals and medication.
  • Researched the market and drafted the customer scenario, with AI assistance.
  • Drafted the business model and cost estimates. These are assumption-based drafts, not validated numbers.
  • Proposed a two-stage rollout: a small pilot first, then a new product if the pilot works.

Teammates' part

My teammates handled the board and model development. I'm not claiming any performance numbers.
