LearningMate

  • Role: PM & Developer
  • Team: 4 people
  • Where: Kookmin University (Global PBL)
  • When: Sep 2026 – present

LearningMate is an AI tutor and learning-support layer for a university Learning Experience Platform (LXP). It sits on top of the existing LMS: inside lecture materials, students get quizzes and a wrong-answer review, so professors can see what students actually understand.

Status: in development. “LearningMate” is a working name. No real users yet. A spring 2027 pilot is our goal, not confirmed. The code is in a private repository. Pull request counts are from GitHub as of Nov 12, 2026.

How we changed direction

  1. Problem

    We started by assuming professors struggled to make lecture materials.

  2. Finding

    Interviews with 6 professors and the university's Center for Teaching and Learning showed that wasn't the real pain. What was hard was knowing how well students understood the material, which made the next class or exam difficult to plan. One professor said it was hard to tell how well students understood before midterms.

  3. Decision

    So we made it easier for students to ask questions. They can take quizzes and keep a wrong-answer notebook right on the lecture-material screen, and professors use those questions to keep tracking understanding.

As a sociology and psychology major, I treated the interviews like field research. We first reviewed research on how professors assess students.

The same gap may also exist at companies, where managers need to know whether their team really understands and agrees with the company's direction — a possible future direction, not something we have tested.

What I did as PM

  • Helped set the scope. With the team and feedback from the teaching center, we focused on the professor side and limited where AI goes.
  • Split the work among the four of us.
  • Planned backward from the Dec 7 final presentation.
  • Agreed on a team PR rule: tests, an independent review, then approval before merging.
