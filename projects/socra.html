Socra: An AI Study Agent That Asks Back (working name)

  • Role: PM & Lead Developer
  • Team: 4 people
  • Where: Kookmin University (Global PBL)
  • When: Sep 2026 – present

Most AI study tools hand students the answer. As PM, I scoped and led a university course platform whose AI tutor asks guiding questions instead, while building much of it myself (35 pull requests across syllabus AI, quiz review dialogue, question collection, and professor dashboards). We interviewed professors and the university's teaching center to shape the product toward a planned pilot.

Status: in development. “Socra” is a working name. A pilot is planned, and there are no real-user results yet. The source code is in a private repository.

What I did as PM

  • Set priorities and scope. We focused on the professor-side experience and narrowed down where AI is added.
  • Split the work across the four of us.
  • Planned backward from the Dec 7 final presentation.
  • Ran a merge rule: test, then review, then my approval before anything is merged.

What I built

  • Syllabus AI assistant
  • CQI report
  • Question collection (a view for gathering questions)
  • Mistake notebook and quiz review connected to the learning AI
  • Professor dashboard for student management

Screens

Screens show the professor-side tool. The interface is in Korean. Early version, Nov 2026; the product has changed since. Student-side screens (the guiding-question chat) are coming soon.
