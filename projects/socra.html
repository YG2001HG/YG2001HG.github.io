Socra: An AI Study Agent That Asks Back (working name)

  • Role: PM & Developer
  • Team: 4 people
  • Where: Kookmin University (Global PBL)
  • When: Sep 2026 – present

Most AI study tools give students the answer. This one asks a question back. I'm PM of the four-person team building it for university courses, and I wrote 35 pull requests (32 merged) across the syllabus assistant, quiz review, question collection and professor dashboard. We interviewed professors and the university's teaching center to decide what to build. A pilot is planned for spring 2027.

Status: in development. “Socra” is a working name. No real users yet; a pilot is planned for spring 2027. The code is in a private repository.

What I did as PM

  • Set the scope. We focused on the professor side and limited where AI goes.
  • Split the work among the four of us.
  • Planned backward from the Dec 7 final presentation.
  • Set a merge rule: tests, review, then my approval.

What I built

  • Syllabus AI assistant
  • CQI report
  • Question collection (a view for gathering questions)
  • Mistake notebook and quiz review, connected to the learning AI
  • Professor dashboard for student management

Screens

Professor-side screens, Korean interface. Early version from Nov 2026; the product has changed since. Student-side screens (the guiding-question chat) are coming soon.
