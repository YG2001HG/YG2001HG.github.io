Also: survey research. Why do people protect their personal data online?
Sociology course paper, 2024. I designed the study, collected the responses, and ran the analysis: an online survey of 121 people in their 20s (Nov 2024) on how much risk of online sexual crime they perceive and how much they protect their personal data. People who saw more risk protected their data more. That link was weaker among people who trust online information more. Limits: small, non-random sample, mostly university students. It shows a pattern, not a cause.
