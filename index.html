03

Survey research: why do people protect their data online?

Sociology course paper · 2024

I designed the study, collected the responses, and ran a regression analysis on how perceived risk of online crime relates to protecting personal data. People who saw more risk protected their data more, and the link was weaker among people who trust online information more. Limits: a small, non-random sample, mostly university students. It shows a pattern, not a cause.
